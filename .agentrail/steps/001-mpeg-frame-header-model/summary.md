Implemented a reusable semantic MPEG Layer III frame-header parser entirely in MLPL over the existing declarative bit-field layer. It supports MPEG-1, MPEG-2, and explicitly labeled de-facto MPEG-2.5 tables; derives CRC presence, bitrate, sample rate, channels, frame samples, and frame byte length; and rejects bad shape/domain, sync, reserved version/bitrate/rate/emphasis, unsupported layers, and indeterminate free bitrate. Added five mlplunit tests and upgraded the narrated demo to contrast 417-byte MPEG-1 and 208-byte MPEG-2.5 frames. Documented ISO/IEC 11172-3, ISO/IEC 13818-3, ITU-R BS.1115-1, and mpg123 extension evidence. Full just check passes with 14 suites.
